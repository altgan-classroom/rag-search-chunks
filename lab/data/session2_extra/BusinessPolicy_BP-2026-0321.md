# Small Business Property Policy BP-2026-0321

Issued by Northgate Insurance. Effective 1 March 2026. Business: bakery and cafe, 22 Market Street.

## 1. What is covered

The policy covers the shop building contents, stock, equipment and loss of income after an insured event.

## 2. Limits and deductibles

| Cover | Limit per claim | Deductible |
|---|---|---|
| Equipment | $80,000 | $1,000 |
| Stock | $40,000 | $1,000 |
| Escape of water | $25,000 | $1,500 |
| Loss of income | $60,000 | 3 days |
| Theft | $30,000 | $1,500 |

## 3. Escape of water

We cover damage to the shop caused by the sudden escape of water from a burst pipe or a failed appliance, up to $25,000 per claim. Cover does not apply where the shop was closed for more than 7 consecutive days without the water supply switched off. Report the loss within 7 days of discovering it.

## 4. Refrigeration

Spoilage of stock after a power cut is covered up to $5,000 if the power was off for more than 12 hours.
