# Claims FAQ 2026

Short answers to the questions we hear most often at Northgate Insurance.

## How long do I have to report a claim?

It depends on the policy. Home policies ask for a report within 14 or 30 days of discovering the loss. Motor claims must be reported within 48 hours. Business policies ask for 7 days. Check section 3 of your policy.

## Which claim code do I use?

Use CL-4471 for water damage, CL-2290 for theft and CL-3015 for glass. Always quote the code and your policy number.

## Will you pay for the full cost of repair?

We pay up to the limit in your policy summary, after the deductible or excess has been taken off.

## Why was my claim refused?

The most common reasons are a missed reporting deadline, damage caused by wear and tear, and an empty property that was left unheated in winter.

## Can I choose my own repairer?

Yes, but we must approve the quote before work starts.
