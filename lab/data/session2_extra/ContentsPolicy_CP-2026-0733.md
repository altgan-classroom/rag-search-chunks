# Contents Insurance Policy CP-2026-0733

Issued by Northgate Insurance. Effective 15 February 2026. For tenants and owners who insure belongings only. Address: Flat 12, Orchard Court.

## 1. What is covered

The policy covers furniture, clothing, appliances and personal electronics inside the home. It does not cover the building itself, fixed fittings or the landlord's property.

## 2. Limits and deductibles

| Cover | Limit per claim | Deductible |
|---|---|---|
| All contents | $35,000 | $250 |
| Single item | $2,500 | $250 |
| Laptops and phones | $3,000 | $350 |
| Escape of water damage to contents | $8,000 | $250 |
| Alternative accommodation | $6,000 | none |

## 3. Escape of water

We pay for damage to your belongings when water escapes suddenly from a burst pipe, tank or washing machine, up to $8,000 per claim. Damage that happens because the home was left empty for more than 30 days in a row is not covered. Report the loss within 14 days.

## 4. Away from home

Belongings are covered anywhere in the world for up to 60 days a year, to a limit of $5,000.
