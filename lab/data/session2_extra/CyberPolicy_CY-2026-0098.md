# Cyber Protection Policy CY-2026-0098

Issued by Northgate Insurance. Effective 1 May 2026. For individuals and home workers.

## 1. What is covered

The policy covers money lost to online fraud, the cost of restoring accounts and devices after a cyber attack, and legal help if your identity is stolen.

## 2. Limits

| Cover | Limit per year | Excess |
|---|---|---|
| Online banking fraud | $15,000 | $100 |
| Ransomware and data restoration | $5,000 | $100 |
| Identity theft legal costs | $10,000 | none |
| Phishing and scam losses | $3,000 | $100 |

## 3. Conditions

You must tell your bank within 24 hours of finding a fraudulent transaction, report the loss to the police within 7 days, and keep your devices and software up to date.

## 4. Exclusions

We do not cover losses caused by sharing a password or a one-time code with another person, or crypto currency investments.
