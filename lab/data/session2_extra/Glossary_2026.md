# Insurance Glossary 2026

Plain-language meanings of words used in Northgate Insurance policies.

## Deductible and excess

The part of a claim you pay yourself before we pay the rest. Home policies say deductible, motor and travel policies say excess. They mean the same thing.

## Escape of water

Water that suddenly leaks or bursts from a pipe, tank or appliance inside a building. Slow leaks and flooding from outside are not escape of water.

## Limit per claim

The most we will pay for one claim under one section of the policy.

## Loss adjuster

An independent expert we send to inspect damage and agree the cost of repair.

## Exclusion

Something the policy does not cover, such as wear and tear or damage caused by neglect.

## Waiting period

The number of days after the policy begins before a type of cover starts.
