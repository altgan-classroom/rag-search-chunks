# Home Insurance Policy HP-2024-0210

Issued by Northgate Insurance. Effective 1 January 2024. Policyholder: S. Menon, 9 Hill Crescent.

## 1. Who is covered

The policy covers the named policyholder and the members of the household who live at the insured address. Cover applies to the main dwelling and fixed fittings.

## 2. Summary of limits

| Cover | Limit per claim | Deductible |
|---|---|---|
| Buildings | $350,000 | $400 |
| Contents | $40,000 | $400 |
| Escape of water | $5,000 | $400 |
| Theft | $10,000 | $800 |

## 3. Escape of water

We cover damage to the buildings and contents caused by the sudden escape of water from a burst pipe, a tank or a fixed appliance, up to the escape of water limit shown in section 2, which is $5,000 per claim. This cover does not apply where the property was left unheated for more than 96 consecutive hours between November and March. You must report the loss within 30 days of discovering it.

## 4. Theft

We cover theft or attempted theft where there is evidence of forced entry. Cover for items kept outside the dwelling is limited to $1,000 in total.

## 5. General exclusions

We do not cover wear and tear, gradual deterioration, or loss from war, nuclear risks or flood from a river or the sea.
