# Pet Insurance Policy PP-2026-0412

Issued by Northgate Insurance. Effective 1 April 2026. Pet: Labrador, age 4, name Bruno.

## 1. What is covered

The policy covers veterinary fees for accidents and illness, third party liability if your dog injures a person, and the cost of boarding if you are in hospital.

## 2. Limits and excess

| Cover | Annual limit | Excess |
|---|---|---|
| Veterinary fees | $6,000 | $120 |
| Dental treatment after an accident | $800 | $120 |
| Third party liability (dog bites) | $500,000 | none |
| Boarding fees | $1,200 | none |
| Lost pet advertising | $300 | none |

## 3. Waiting periods

Illness cover starts 14 days after the policy begins. Accident cover starts the next day.

## 4. Exclusions

We do not cover pre-existing conditions, routine vaccinations, neutering, or injuries from racing or fighting.

## 5. Claims

Send the vet invoice and the clinical notes within 90 days of treatment. We pay the vet directly if you ask us to.
