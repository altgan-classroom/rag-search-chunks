# Travel Insurance Policy TP-2025-0188

Issued by Northgate Insurance. Effective 1 June 2025. Single trip, 14 days, destination: Singapore.

## 1. What is covered

Emergency medical costs, trip cancellation for insured reasons, delayed baggage and personal liability.

## 2. Limits and excess

| Cover | Limit | Excess |
|---|---|---|
| Emergency medical | $250,000 | $75 |
| Trip cancellation | $4,000 | $75 |
| Baggage and personal items | $1,500 | $75 |
| Delayed baggage (after 6 hours) | $200 | none |
| Personal liability | $1,000,000 | none |

## 3. What is not covered

Cancellation because you changed your mind is not covered. Losses caused by flood, storm or earthquake are covered only if you bought the natural events extension. Claims for lost items need a police or airline report within 24 hours.
